## 3. HOW THE FOUR PACKS COMPLEMENT EACH OTHER

There is a coherent theme: **scale and logistics.**

- **Plains & Prairies** supplies big-acreage field equipment — 18–25.6 m implements, 88,000 l auger wagon, 600+ HP tractors
- **Mercedes-Benz Trucks** supplies the transport layer — 39,500 l trailers at 90 km/h, plus Unimog implement carriers that travel at road speed
- **NEXAT** consolidates the field fleet into one 1,100 HP carrier with swappable modules
- **Highlands Fishing** is the odd one out — a full content expansion rather than equipment

⭐ **Plains & Prairies + Mercedes together are a genuine pairing:** the Brandt 2500 DXT auger wagon
(88,000 l) feeding sirch UniBox/AgroBox trailers (39,500 l each) at 90 km/h is a complete
harvest-logistics chain.

⚠️ **NEXAT partly overlaps Plains & Prairies** — both address large-field work, and the NEXAT
carrier replaces conventional tractor-plus-implement setups. Buying the pass gets you both
approaches; you'll likely commit to one.

---

## 4. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **Buy the pass if you want Highlands Fishing plus anything else.** At $29.99 for the expansion alone versus $39.99 for all four, the other three packs cost $10 combined.
- **Buy Highlands Fishing standalone** if you only care about the new map, crop, animal and fishing — and have no interest in big-acreage American equipment, trucks, or the NEXAT system.
- **Skip the pass** if you play on small or irregular fields. Three of the four packs are built around large-scale equipment that's awkward on small plots.
- ⭐ **This is the pass with the tutorials.** Highlands Fishing is the only FS25 DLC with dedicated Academy coverage, which makes it the most learnable content in the pass.

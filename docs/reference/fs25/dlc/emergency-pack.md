## 1. ⭐ WHAT IT ADDS

Emergency-response gameplay layered onto the farm. Officially framed as playing "a
community-involved farmer who dispatches firefighting equipment to handle crises."

⭐ **Ten emergencies across four different scenarios**, on and around local farmlands.

### The four activity types (official, news_id=690)

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Firefighting** | "You extinguish burning **pallets, containers, bales and fields**." Both **manual (by hand) and vehicle-based** firefighting supported |
| ⭐ **Technical rescue & hazmat** | Car crashes, removing road obstructions, "secure **oil spills** and respond to technical rescue situations" |
| **Road clearing** | Removing obstructions from roads |
| ⭐ **Animal rescue** | "cats need saving from trees" |

⭐ **Multiplayer: the fire trucks seat up to NINE players.** This is the standout feature, and makes
the pack a co-op set-piece rather than a solo activity. *(No other FS25 source documents vehicle
seat counts, so this can't be ranked against other content.)*

**[SECONDARY — FS Wiki]** restates the four activity types and adds: "Extinguish rising flames by
hand or from the safety of the fire engine."

## 3. HOW IT RELATES TO THE REST OF THE GAME

This DLC is **orthogonal to farming** — it adds no crops, no animals, no map, no production chains,
and doesn't interact with any mechanic in the other reference files. It is a self-contained activity
layer.

The one connection worth noting: ⭐ **fields can burn.** Officially, firefighting covers "pallets,
containers, bales and **fields**." If field fires can occur outside missions, that's a risk to
standing crops — but **nothing official states whether field fires occur spontaneously or only as
scripted mission events.**

---

## 4. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **It's free and it's optional.** Nothing in it is required for farming; nothing in farming requires it.
- ⭐ **The nine-player truck capacity is the reason to install it** if you play multiplayer. For solo play the appeal is ten scripted missions.
- **Only two functional vehicles.** With 320 HP and 231 HP at 100 km/h, both trucks are fast road vehicles — they're not usable as farm machinery in any documented way.
- ⚠️ **Requires Update 1.20**, the highest update requirement of any FS25 DLC documented here (alongside SKY Agriculture). Check your game version.

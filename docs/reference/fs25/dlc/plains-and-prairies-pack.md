## 1. WHAT IT ADDS

North-American big-acreage equipment. The theme is **scale** — this pack contains some of the
largest-capacity implements in the game.

⭐ **Three small mechanics improvements** ship with it (stated on the official page):

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trailer covers** | "Realistic trailer covers roll over your stored crops" |
| **Lift axles** | Working lift axles on manure tankers |
| **Pneumatic sprayers** | Pneumatic sprayers for **solid fertilizer** application |

Ford is introduced as "a pioneer of industrialization." Note the pack contains **paired Ford and
Versatile badge variants** of the same two tractors (976 and 1156) — historically accurate, but it
means 21 machines include 2 duplicates in all but name.

## 4. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **This is a big-field pack.** Almost everything demands 350–600 HP. On small or awkward fields most of it is unusable, and the 18–25.6 m working widths waste time on short runs.
- ⭐ **Power budget is the constraint.** The Salford harrow (600 HP), Seed Hawk (550 HP) and Brandt auger wagon (500 HP) all need more than most base-game tractors provide. The **DeltraTrack (583–665 HP)** is the only machine in the pack that can pull all three.
- ⭐ **Watch the 20 km/h tractors.** The Ford/Versatile 976 and 1156 and Big Roy are all capped at 20 km/h — fine in-field, painful on road transport between distant fields.
- **The Ford and Versatile variants are cosmetically distinct, mechanically identical.** Buy one, not both.
- **The slurry capacity here is exceptional** — the GEA STR-447 at 39,800 l and the EL48-8D-7900 at 35,900 l make large-scale manure logistics practical, which pairs well with `precision-farming-3.md` (manure first, then mineral top-up) and with a large cow or pig operation.
- **No new crops, animals, map or gameplay mechanics** — this is purely equipment plus three cosmetic/physics touches.

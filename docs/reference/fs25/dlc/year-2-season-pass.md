## 4. HOW THE THREE PACKS RELATE

Unlike Year 1's coherent scale-and-logistics theme, **Year 2 is thematically scattered**:

- **Vredo** adds two ongoing *costs* — grassland now degrades, corn now attracts boars — plus slurry injection as an upgrade
- **SKY Agriculture** is a pure seedbed pack — power harrows, combined drills, and the standout **X50+ Econov** spreader (50 m at only 150 HP)
- **Beans & Alpacas** is a full content expansion, unrelated to the other two

⭐ **The one genuine synergy:** Vredo's **slurry injectors** and SKY's **fertilizer spreaders** both
serve the fertilising step, and both matter more under Precision Farming, where application method
and order affect the Environmental Score (`precision-farming-3.md` §2, §7).

## 5. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **The value case rests almost entirely on Beans & Alpacas** — 77% of the price, and still unreleased. If that expansion doesn't appeal, the pass is $34.99 for two modest packs.
- ⭐ **Buy the pass if you want Beans & Alpacas at all.** At $26.99 standalone versus $34.99 for everything, Vredo and SKY cost **$8.00 combined** — roughly the price of Vredo ($7.99) on its own, making SKY effectively free.
- ⭐ **The X50+ Econov (SKY) is the best single machine in this pass** — 50 m working width for 150 HP is the strongest width-per-horsepower ratio documented across all these files.
- ⚠️ **Vredo's machines are fully specced, but its mechanics aren't.** Grassland degradation rate, overseeding restoration and boar damage magnitude are unpublished, so the gameplay value can't be judged from sources — though the 12 machines (incl. a 710 HP / 32,000 l self-propelled slurry tanker) are documented.
- ⚠️ **Requires Update 1.14+**, and SKY Agriculture separately requires **1.20+**. Check your version.
- **Compare with Year 1** (`year-1-season-pass.md`): that pass has more items (88 vs 50+), more new brands (10 vs 3), and the only tutorial-documented content in the game.

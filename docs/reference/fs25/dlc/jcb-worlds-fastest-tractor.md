## 1. WHAT IT ADDS

⭐ **A single vehicle — the real-world Guinness World Record holder for fastest tractor.**

| Machine | Shop category | Power | Top speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **JCB World's Fastest Tractor** | **Miscellaneous** | ⭐ **1,016 HP** | ⭐ **247 km/h (153 mph)** |

⚠️ **Note the shop category: Miscellaneous, not Tractors.** That strongly suggests it is not
intended as a working machine — but the page does not state whether it can attach implements.

### Official description

"A British-engineered vehicle that holds the Guinness World Record for top speed. The tractor is
powered by a highly-modified six-cylinder **JCB Dieselmax 7.2-litre engine** producing over
**1,016 horsepower**, combined with **hybrid boost technology** pairing a larger turbo with an
electrically driven supercharger."

⚠️ **The page lists no implements, tools, buildings or placeables** — this single vehicle is the
entire DLC.

## 2. CONTEXT — how fast is 247 km/h?

Against every other vehicle documented across these files:

| Vehicle | Top speed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **JCB World's Fastest Tractor** | **247 km/h** | This DLC |
| Skoda Octavia | 140 km/h | Highlands Fishing |
| Schlingmann fire trucks | 100 km/h | Emergency Pack |
| Mercedes trucks; **Unimogs** *(Medium Tractors category)* | 90 km/h | Mercedes-Benz Trucks Pack |
| JCB Fastrac 8000 iCON | 70 km/h | Highlands Fishing |
| Most modern tractors | 40–60 km/h | Various |
| NEXAT carrier | 25 km/h | NEXAT Pack |
| Versatile "Big Roy" | 20 km/h | Plains & Prairies |

⭐ **It is roughly 3.5× faster than the fastest conventional tractor in the game** (the JCB Fastrac
8000 iCON at 70 km/h), ~2.7× the Unimogs — which are tractor-category but road-speed at 90 km/h —
and nearly twice the speed of the fastest road car documented.

At **1,016 HP** it also out-powers everything except the NEXAT carrier (1,100 HP) — but the NEXAT
does useful work, and this almost certainly does not.

## 3. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **This is a novelty purchase, not a farming tool.** The "Miscellaneous" shop category and the complete absence of implements point to it being a toy.
- **At $1.99 it's the second-cheapest *paid* DLC** (behind the CR11 Gold Edition at $0.99; three others are free), so the cost of finding out is trivial.
- ⭐ **Possible practical use: getting across a large map fast.** On a big map with distant fields, a 247 km/h vehicle beats walking or driving a 40 km/h tractor — the same role the Skoda Octavia or a Can-Am fills in Highlands Fishing, only faster.
- ⚠️ **Do not assume it can pull implements.** Nothing official states it can, and the shop category suggests otherwise. Verify in-game.
- **Requires Update 1.14** — check your version.

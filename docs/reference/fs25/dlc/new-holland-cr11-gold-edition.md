## 1. WHAT IT ADDS

⭐ **A gold paint variant of the flagship New Holland combine and its header.** Officially described
as "the unique New Holland CR11 Gold Edition with a **unique gold paint finish**."

| Machine | Category | Power | Top speed | Capacity | Working width |

| ⭐ **New Holland CR11 Gold Edition** | Harvesters | **775 HP** | 30 km/h | ⭐ **20,000 l** | — |
| **New Holland FD250 FlexDraper® Gold Edition** | Grain Headers | — | — | — | ⭐ **15.2 m** |

Official framing: "one of the flagship combines, featuring authentic, highly detailed digitization
based on the real machine. With its high capacity, power and efficiency, the CR11 will help you
achieve higher productivity on your virtual fields."

⚠️ **The page does not state whether the base (non-gold) CR11 is already in the base game.** The
wording implies this is a cosmetic edition of an existing flagship machine rather than new
capability. Verify before assuming it unlocks a machine you don't have.

## 2. CONTEXT — where this sits

At **775 HP / 20,000 l**, the CR11 is the largest-capacity **standalone combine** documented across
these files:

| Harvester | Power | Capacity | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **New Holland CR11** | **775 HP** | ⭐ **20,000 l** | This DLC |
| NEXAT NEXCO *(module, not a standalone combine)* | — | 32,500 l | NEXAT Pack |
| Grimme VARITRON 470 (root crops) | 460 HP | 9,600 l | Highlands Fishing |

The **FD250 FlexDraper at 15.2 m** matches the widest grain headers in the game — the GERINGHOFF
XtremeFlex Razor 50FT and Patriot RotaDisc in the NEXAT Pack are also 15.2 m, and MacDon's FD140
reaches 12.5 m.

⭐ **A 20,000 l tank matters practically:** per `crops-101.md` §6.1, "the tank will fill up quickly"
with starter combines, forcing frequent trailer runs. A 20,000 l tank drastically cuts unloading
stops on large fields.

## 3. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **$0.99 for a cosmetic finish.** If you already own the CR11 in the base game, this buys you gold paint and nothing else.
- ⭐ **If the CR11 is NOT in your base game**, this is extraordinary value — a 775 HP / 20,000 l flagship combine plus a 15.2 m header for under a dollar. ⚠️ **Verify which case applies before buying.**
- **Only useful with grain-group crops.** The FD250 is a **grain header** — per `crops-101.md` §2 that covers wheat, sorghum, barley, oat, canola, soybeans and long grain rice. It won't handle corn, sunflowers, root crops or specialty crops.
- **775 HP is a lot of machine for a small farm.** On short rows a 15.2 m header wastes most of its width on turns; this pays off on large, regular fields.
- **Requires only Update 1.1** — the lowest update requirement of any FS25 DLC documented here, so version compatibility is unlikely to be an issue.

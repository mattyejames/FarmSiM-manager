## 1. WHAT IT ADDS — THE BIG PICTURE

This is a **full expansion**, not a machine pack. Four distinct additions:

| Addition | Detail | Covered in |
|---|---|---|
| **New map: Kinlaig** | Scotland-inspired — rocky valleys, rolling green hills, open waters, overcast skies, castles, new construction projects | — |
| **New crop: onions** | With specialised planting/harvesting equipment | `crops-101.md` §6.3 |
| **New animal: highland cattle** | Breeding-only, no milk | `animals-101.md` |
| **New mechanic: fishing & aquaculture** | Rod fishing + salmon/trout farming | `fishing-aquaculture.md` |

⭐ **Planning note: the four additions interlock.** Fish feed requires **flour + soybeans + oil** —
all arable products. Onions process into **fried onions (with oil), onion salt and soup**. So a
grain-and-oilseed farm feeds both the new fish chain and the new crop chain.

### Key mechanics summary (full detail in the linked files)

**Onions** — plant Mar–Apr, harvest Aug–Sep. ⭐ **Three-machine harvest**: onion harvester (front,
cuts foliage and digs) → onion windrower (rear, cleans and rows) → second harvester (picks up).
⭐ Optional **Holaras AS 25** onion topper placeable — pack onions with it **for more profit**.

**Highland cattle** — breeding only, no milk. Mature at **18 months**. ⭐ **The meadow planted in
the fence covers up to 40% of feeding.** ⭐ To house dairy cows and highland cattle together you
need a **dairy barn**.

**Fishing** — rod from the **hand tools** section. Hotspots around Kinlaig. Longer casts = faster
bites; shallow water = slower.

**Aquaculture** — ⭐ **Salmon are offshore-only; trout can use a fish lake OR offshore.** Start with
trout in a lake — no cargo vessel needed. Fish feed = **flour + soybeans + oil** (olive, canola,
rice or sunflower all accepted).

## 4. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **Best-value purchase for gameplay depth.** At $29.99 it's the most expensive FS25 DLC, but it adds a map, a crop, an animal and an entire new mechanic — versus machine packs that add equipment only.
- ⭐ **Three JCB Fastracs** span 130–348 HP, and the 8000 iCON tops out at **70 km/h** — the fastest conventional tractor in this pack. Useful for long hauls on a map with distant fields.
- **The DAMMANN Profi-Class Tridem (42 m, 20,000 l)** is a very large sprayer — worth noting against the base game's options if you run big fields.
- **Onion equipment is the only reason to buy the Holaras machines** — they're single-purpose.
- **The four HÖRMANN large barns** matter for the highland-cattle-plus-dairy-cow constraint: you need a dairy barn to mix the two species.
- **Start with trout, not salmon.** Salmon force the offshore aquaculture path and its cargo vessel; trout work in a simple fish lake.

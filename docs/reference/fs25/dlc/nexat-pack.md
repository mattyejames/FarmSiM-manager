## 1. ⭐ THE CORE MECHANIC — one vehicle, swappable modules

NEXAT = "**Next Generation Agriculture Technology**" — described officially as "the world's first
**holistic crop production system**," built around "a strong, versatile, and **wide-span carrier
vehicle**."

⭐ **The mechanic in one line (official):** *"NEXAT is based on **one vehicle for all major crop
processing steps**. Integrate individual modules from renowned manufacturers for different field
processes like **tillage, sowing, plant protection, and harvesting**"* — turning conventional
implements into **self-propelled implements**.

Stated benefits: "reduce the total number of required machines" and handle "**multiple tasks in one
pass**."

⭐ **This is a fleet-consolidation play, not a capability upgrade.** Instead of a tractor plus a
harvester plus a sprayer plus a slurry tanker, you buy one 1,100 HP carrier and swap modules onto
it. The trade-off is that the carrier can only do one job at a time.

## 3. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **The working widths cluster at 13.5–15.2 m.** That's the NEXAT system's natural field footprint — it's designed for large, regular fields. On small or irregular plots the carrier is unwieldy.
- ⭐ **One carrier, one job at a time.** The consolidation saving is real but it serialises your work: you can't be sowing one field while harvesting another with the same machine. On a large operation you may still need conventional machines alongside.
- **Capacities are large across the board** — 32,500 l harvester module, 30,000 l slurry, 22,000 l sprayer, 20,500 l seed hopper. Fewer refill trips is a genuine time saving.
- ⚠️ **25 km/h** makes road transit slow. Best suited to a farm with contiguous fields.
- **The Väderstad Carrier NX + Tempo NX27 combination** is the one-pass cultivate-and-seed setup — worth noting against `ground-working-101.md` §11, where direct sowing machines are already flagged as saving a working step (and, under Precision Farming, scoring maximum tillage points).

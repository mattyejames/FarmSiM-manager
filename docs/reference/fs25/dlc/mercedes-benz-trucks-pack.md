## 1. WHAT IT ADDS

Road transport and the classic Mercedes tractor line. ⭐ **This is the transport-and-logistics
pack** — nearly everything here moves goods at 90 km/h rather than working fields.

Two distinct groups:

| Group | What it is |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **MB-trac (4 models)** | Classic 1970s–80s system tractors, 65–180 HP, all capped at **40 km/h** |
| ⭐ **Unimog (2) + Arocs/Actros (4) + Heizotruck** | Modern trucks and implement carriers, 180–625 HP, **90 km/h** |

configurations clear the 80 HP needed for the KRONE Comprima round baler in
`straw-harvest-pack.md`; the base 65 HP unit does not.

### Unimog — implement carriers

| Machine | Category | Power | Top speed | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unimog U 1800-2400** | Medium Tractors | 180–241 HP | ⭐ **90 km/h** | 2,200 l |

⭐ **The Unimogs are the interesting machines here.** They are classed as **Medium Tractors** —
so they take implements — but run at **90 km/h** with a built-in load capacity. That combination
doesn't exist elsewhere: a tool carrier that also travels at truck speed.

## 4. PLANNING NOTES

- ⭐ **Buy this for logistics, not fieldwork.** Its value is moving large volumes fast — 39,500 l at 90 km/h. If your bottleneck is hauling harvest to distant selling points, this pack addresses it directly.
- ⚠️ **The TECTRUM variants are undocumented.** They list identical specs to the plain Arocs and Actros L, and no source states what TECTRUM adds. Check in-game before assuming a capability.
- ⭐ **The Unimogs are the most versatile single purchase** — implement-capable at 90 km/h with onboard capacity, spanning 180–354 HP.
- **The MB-tracs are collector machines.** 40 km/h and 65–180 HP make them charming but unremarkable next to modern tractors; buy them for the aesthetic, not the capability.
- **Pairs well with Plains & Prairies** — that pack's Brandt 2500 DXT auger wagon (88,000 l) and these trailers together make a serious grain-logistics operation.
- ⚠️ **No prices or weights** are published for any item.
- **Nothing here interacts with crops, animals or production chains** — it's pure transport capability.

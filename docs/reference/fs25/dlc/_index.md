## 5. BUYING GUIDANCE

**Install first (free, no downside):**
1. ⭐ **Precision Farming 3.0** — deepest mechanical change in the game
2. ⭐ **Straw Harvest** — monetises a by-product you already produce
3. **Emergency Pack** — optional activity layer, 9-player co-op

**Best value if you want depth:** ⭐ **Highlands Fishing** ($29.99) — map + crop + animal + a whole
new mechanic, and the only DLC with Academy tutorials. Or the **Year 1 Season Pass** ($39.99),
which adds three more packs for effectively $10.

**Cheapest paid DLC / best single machine per dollar:** ⭐ **New Holland CR11 Gold** ($0.99) — *if* the CR11 isn't
already in your base game. Verify first.

**Skip unless you have large fields:** Plains & Prairies and NEXAT are both built around 14–25 m
implements and 350–665 HP tractors.

**Wait and see:** ⚠️ **Beans & Alpacas** (unreleased). ⚠️ **Vredo**'s machines are fully specced,
but all three of its gameplay mechanics are unquantified — its value can't be judged from sources.
